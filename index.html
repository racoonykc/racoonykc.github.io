Kaicheng Yang

Undergraduate Researcher · Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Biography

I am an undergraduate researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I am also an incoming PhD student at SphereLab, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I will work with Prof. Weiyang Liu (刘威杨). My previous work was with Prof. Yulun Zhang (张宇伦).

My research explores the transfer, connection, and carriers of intelligence. I study model quantization as a way to understand how intelligence can be represented in discrete systems, and I am interested in future interfaces that connect machine intelligence with human intelligence.

Research

My current work begins with discretizing intelligence: identifying which structures survive when continuous models are represented with very few bits. I am interested in where this perspective leads next, including the bandwidth and interfaces between biological and machine intelligence.
